## LinkedIn

not broken. not slow. sitting eighteen pixels under the bottom of the screen, on the exact laptop most of you are reading this on.

i found it today. by accident. while measuring something else entirely.

so every one of those impressions landed on a page where the one thing i wanted them to do was invisible unless they scrolled, and i spent two weeks celebrating the number that counts how many people saw nothing.

what i am embarrassed about is that for two weeks of it, nobody could find the button.

here is the part this app does not want to hear. reach is not traction and it is not a leading indicator of traction. i had two posts do 20,000 each and what sixty five thousand impressions converted into was a page that did not work on a macbook air.

you can go viral with a broken funnel. you just go viral into a bucket with a hole in it, and the dopamine is identical, which is exactly why nobody checks.

i only checked because i was changing something unrelated. 738 pixels against a 720 pixel fold. eighteen pixels of nothing, twice a week, for a month.

fixed it in an hour. then measured it at six screen sizes, because i have stopped trusting anything i have not opened myself.

if you post every day and the numbers look great and nothing is happening: go and open your own site on a laptop. not your monitor. a laptop, with a bookmarks bar, like a person has.

i am not going to stop posting. i am going to stop confusing it with progress.

anyway. eighteen pixels.
