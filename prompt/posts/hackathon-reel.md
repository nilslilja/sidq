# HACKATHON REEL

No voiceover. Text on screen only.
Everything in "quotes" is what you literally type on screen.

Total length: 42 seconds. Never go over 60.


═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

0:00 - 0:03
  SHOT   walking toward the venue, handheld, movement in frame
  TYPE   "i got rejected from this hackathon"

  SHOT   same shot keeps running

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
TEXT STYLE
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Font        bold sans serif. CapCut default bold is fine
Colour      white
Shadow      slight drop shadow or thin dark outline, so it reads on any shot
Size        big. it should feel slightly too big
Position    centred, or lower third
Case        lowercase, exactly as written above
Animation   none. text appears and cuts. no typewriter, no fade

Never put text in the bottom 250px or the top 150px. The app UI covers it.


═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
MUSIC
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Yes, you need music. A silent text reel is dead on arrival.

Use a track from Instagram's own audio library with the trending arrow on it.
Trending audio gets pushed harder than uploaded audio. That is free reach.

Look for:
  - dark, tense, building. not happy, not lo-fi
  - a clear drop around the 30 second mark
  - instrumental. lyrics fight your text

Land the drop exactly on "i wasn't supposed to be in the building".
That line is the reason the video exists.

Audio mix:
  Music          100%
  Original audio 10-20% underneath, not muted

Keeping a little ambient room noise under the music makes it feel real instead
of like a slideshow. Do not mute your clips entirely.


═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
EDITING
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

App          CapCut
Ratio        9:16
Resolution   1080 x 1920
Export       1080p, 60fps, highest bitrate

Hard cuts only. No transitions. No fades. No zoom effects. No filters.
Cut on the beat everywhere except the hold at 0:29.

Missing footage for a slot?
Use any moving shot and put the text over it. The text carries the story, the
footage is texture. Never leave a beat out just because the shot is imperfect.


═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
THE THREE RULES
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

1. First 3 seconds decide everything.
   Put your best walking-in shot at the very front even if it breaks
   chronological order.

2. Everything moves except 0:29.
   Stopping dead on that one line is what makes it land.

3. No motivational outro.
   Nothing like "so if someone told you no this week".
   That single addition breaks the whole character.
