every ai you use has amnesia and you decided that's normal.

you explain your project to claude. it gets it. thread ends.
you open chatgpt. you explain it again.
cursor. again.
gemini. again.

my own machine says i have done that across 6 different tools. 30 conversations.
75 hours. one of them has 45 hours of work sitting inside it.

none of them can read each other. that is not a technical limit. that is the
business model.

so i built sidq.

it reads every ai conversation on your mac. claude, chatgpt, cursor, codex,
gemini, all of it. one keystroke and whatever you were doing lands in whichever
one you open next. word for word, not summarised.

nothing uploads. it never leaves the machine.

free until it works. then twenty.
